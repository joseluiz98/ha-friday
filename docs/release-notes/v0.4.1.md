# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.1: she is heard first, and she announces

This is an **alpha** pre-release. It goes to the alpha satellite, the server and the integration first. Stable devices get it after the 48 h soak and the promotion gate below. v0.4.0 keeps its own soak clock and is promoted on its own schedule.

- When the satellite speaks on its own (a timer, a reminder, the presentation of a new satellite), people talking or the TV in the room no longer cut her: only an active wake word does.
- When she decides not to answer, the voice no longer speaks the marker aloud in any of its forms.
- The satellites can now speak the house announcements ("the washer finished", "coffee is ready"): a chime, then the fixed text in her voice. Nothing is paraphrased and no conversation opens.
- Every release now carries the satellite's top-level sounds, so a satellite that predates a tone gets it.

## New features
- **Timers: only an active wake word interrupts her.** After the first alarm loop she speaks the timer with the microphone closed to the conversation, as reminders already did since 0.3.7. The wake-word detector keeps running: any active word cuts her and opens the microphone; shadow words do not. When she stops (1.5 s of silence) the microphone opens for your answer. Room speech while she speaks does not count as an answer, so if nobody replies the alarm comes back as before (up to 5 min); the wake word that cuts her does count.
- **Presentation of a new satellite: the microphone opens only when she waits for you.** Every speech of hers in the onboarding conversation is gated the same way, so a TV or a conversation in the room no longer derails the setup (#38). The ring shows "speaking" while she talks and "listening" when the microphone opens.

- **Announcements on the satellites: `friday.announce`.** `message` (1 to 500 characters) and an optional `device_id` list of satellite devices; with no device, every F.R.I.D.A.Y. satellite device in Home Assistant's device registry. A device that is not a satellite is refused ("Only satellites can announce."). It returns the per-satellite answers (`vou_tocar`, `na_fila`, `calado`, `expirado`, `sem_satelite`, `sem_suporte`, `erro`, `reenviando`) as the service response and writes one logbook line per call, also when it fails ("Announcement failed: <code>"). A satellite the server does not know answers `sem_satelite` and the others still play. Meant to be called with `continue_on_error: true` from house scripts, next to the Echo announcement.
- **The satellite decides when to play an announcement.** It waits while a conversation is open, a timer alarm is ringing, a wake word is being claimed or a delivery is being opened, and plays when she is free (order: alarms, then deliveries already queued, then announcements). It waits at most 5 minutes from the request: the bot sends the age of the request on every attempt, so a late resend is not played (`expirado`). In do-not-disturb it is dropped (not queued). With the microphone muted it still plays. While the satellite boots it is held until the boot finishes.
- **Chime, alert volume and ducking.** Chime (`sons.anuncio`) then speech, both at the alert volume. Music on the MK1 is ducked as for a reminder. Several announcements play in order, one chime each. When it ends she goes back to rest: no microphone, no "listening" light. An active wake word cuts the announcement and opens a normal conversation; any button stops it, with no conversation; room speech does not stop it.
- **Same text, no new cost.** The speech is cached on the VM (key = voice + TTS model + speaking rate + text, 200 files, oldest out), so a repeated announcement does not call the TTS again.
- **Voice, TTS model and speaking rate in one place.** `voice_settings.tts` is the single source for every TTS call (announcements and the whisper mode). The voice is the one the conversation uses, and the speaking rate (slow / normal / fast) set in Home Assistant applies to announcements too.
- **Whisper replies now follow the speaking rate set in Home Assistant.** Before, a whispered reply always used the normal rate.

## Fixes
- **Pi-opened conversations start with the microphone closed.** The 0.3.7 rule closed it only at her first word, so room speech between the connection and that word still reached the voice, in reminders too.
- **Room speech during her speech no longer ends the conversation.** The satellite's own voice detector still hears the room while the microphone is closed; that speech no longer arms or clears deadlines, counts toward the long-speech cap, or makes her next speech replay after a wake-word cut. Before, a TV talking through a timer or the presentation closed the conversation about 20 s in.
- **Speech gate catches every "no speech" marker.** A one-week review of the gate (12–19/11) found it costs no delay: 183 answers, all released after 0 ms. But 3 turns spoken as `<no speech detected>` or a cut `<no speech` reached the speaker, because the gate matched only `<no speech>` exactly. It now drops any turn that starts with `<no speech`. Normal answers are still released at once (#37).

## Changes by area
- **Satellite program:** `Maestro` gates the timer conversation and every speech of the presentation (`_fechar_mic_do_recado`, which now also closes the microphone right after `conectar`); gated room speech is ignored for deadlines and for `pessoa_falou`. Announcement queue and playback in the maestro, `/announce` on the local port (body is the WAV, `X-Announcement-Id` and `X-Announcement-Age` headers, 16 MB cap), files in `~/anuncios/<id>.wav` removed after playing or expiring, and an id the satellite already holds is not queued twice. New sound `sons.anuncio` (`anuncio_elevenlabs.wav`, with a synthetic `anuncio_a.wav` as a fallback). No migration, no new setting: the new sound key appears by itself.
- **Voice gateway (VM):** `PortaoDeFala` matches the `<no speech` prefix. New `POST /announce`: validates the text and the satellites, makes the speech with the Gemini TTS (resampled to 48 kHz stereo), caches it, sends it to each satellite in parallel (cable IP, then Wi-Fi IP) and answers within 18 s. A satellite that did not answer is retried every 15 s for up to 5 minutes (connection errors only; a 404 or a 4xx is final). One NDJSON line per announcement in the deliveries log (`tipo: "anuncio"`, with the cache hit or miss and the TTS time), one per satellite outcome (`tipo: "anuncio_final"`), and the TTS call goes into the cost log. TTS settings are centralized in `voice_settings.tts`; the model comes from `FRIDAY_TTS_MODEL` (`FRIDAY_WHISPER_TTS_MODEL` is still accepted).
- **Server agent (VM host):** new `announce` command that posts to the bot with a bearer token kept in `/srv/friday/state/announce.token` (or `FRIDAY_TOKEN_ANNOUNCE`, as in the bot; created on the first command, mode 600, never printed). A token problem fails that command only (`token_unavailable`), never the agent. `announce` runs off the agent's receive loop, so a slow announcement does not hold the connection; every other command keeps its arrival order.
- **Integration:** new `friday.announce` service (schema, satellites resolved from the device registry, 30 s wait for the server, response relayed, logbook line on success and on failure).
- **Packaging:** every release bundle now carries the top-level `satellite/sounds/` (12 files plus the new chime, about 9 MB), not only `onboarding/`.
- **Repository tooling:** the pre-push secret sweep reads the history through one `git cat-file --batch`, skips values a file cannot contain, and keeps a local cache of files already proven clean (invalidated by any rule change). Same findings as before, proven against a frozen copy of the old sweep; a push goes from over 4 minutes to seconds.

## Known limits
- Announcements: do-not-disturb turned on while one is playing lets it finish; a bot restart loses its pending retries; an id re-sent after it finished playing plays again; the TTS model has no price yet in the cost table, so its cost is logged unpriced.
- A satellite on an older version answers 404 to announcements and is logged as `sem_suporte`; the MK2 stays so until this release is promoted to stable.

## Promotion gate (stable)
- [ ] In the conversation log, any turn starting with `<no speech` is recorded as `dropped`, and none is heard.
- [ ] `heldMs` on released answers stays near 0.
- [ ] On the alpha satellite, a timer that fires with people talking (or the TV on) is spoken to the end; an active wake word cuts it and opens the microphone.
- [ ] A reminder delivered with room speech is not cut, and the microphone opens when she finishes.
- [ ] Nobody answering a timer while the room talks: the alarm comes back after she finishes.
- [ ] On the MK1 at rest, an announcement plays: chime, then speech, at the alert volume.
- [ ] During a conversation it waits, then plays when the conversation ends.
- [ ] In do-not-disturb it is dropped: the answer is `calado` and no file is left in `~/anuncios`.
- [ ] Sent during a reboot, it is held and plays after the boot finishes.
- [ ] An active wake word cuts it and opens a conversation; any button stops it.
- [ ] The same text twice: the second one is a cache hit in the bot log.
- [ ] With the speaking rate set to slow and to fast, she does not read "Fale devagar:" or "Fale rápido:" aloud, in announcements and in whispered replies.
- [ ] An announcement whose text contains a wake word ("sexta-feira") does not cut itself.
- [ ] Music playing on the MK1 is ducked during the announcement and comes back after it.
- [ ] A satellite that predates the release gets the new chime file after the update.

## All changes
- Speech gate drops every "<no speech" marker variant (#37)
- Timer and presentation speech: only an active wake word interrupts her (#38)
- Announcements on the satellites: `friday.announce`, the `announce` server command, `POST /announce` on the bot, the satellite queue and the chime `sons.anuncio`; TTS settings centralized; release bundles carry the top-level satellite sounds (#40)
- Faster pre-push secret sweep with identical findings (#41)
