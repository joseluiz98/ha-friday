# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.7: the living-room satellite hears the person who called her, and updates arrive again

This release teaches the XVF3800 satellites to listen only to whoever said "Friday" while the TV talks, and fixes the update that never reached the office satellite. It carries everything in v0.4.6, which no satellite received.

- In a conversation, TV speech no longer reaches her, no longer starts a follow-up and no longer keeps the conversation open.
- A new **Learn TV direction** button tells the satellite where the TV is.
- A release that leaves the server's update agent unchanged reaches the satellites again, and a withheld package is reported after an hour.
- From v0.4.6: the satellite no longer ends a conversation while you are still speaking, or as you start a new sentence.

## New features
- **Two-beam listening on XVF3800 satellites.** During a conversation the board aims one beam at the person who said the wake word and another at the TV. Each moment of sound is judged as the caller, the TV or unclear from the speech energy of the two beams. Only the caller's speech opens, holds and ends a turn, and the satellite, not the server, decides when your turn ends. (#62)
  - The beam on the caller lasts the whole conversation and follows the caller to another spot in the room at each follow-up.
  - A turn ends when the caller's voice stops for 1.5 s, even if the TV goes on. Any single turn ends after 60 s; the conversation goes on.
  - It falls back to the v0.4.6 behaviour when the caller's direction cannot be confirmed, when the conversation did not start with the wake word, in whispered conversations, with **Only the wake word interrupts** off, and on Jabra satellites.
  - Advanced settings, per satellite: **Two-beam listening** (on by default), **TV direction**, **TV elevation**, **Caller margin**, **TV margin**, **Caller silence**, **Turn ceiling**.
- **Learn TV direction.** With the TV playing speech and nobody talking, press the button on the satellite's device page. The satellite measures the TV's direction and height and stores them, or refuses with the reason: too little sound, no stable direction, too close to where callers usually stand. The result arrives as the **TV direction learn** event. (#62)
- **Voice check, recording only.** The server compares the caller's voice with the voices taught on that satellite and records what it would have done. It never cuts anything in this release: it waits for data from real use before it is trusted. (#62)

## Fixes
- **v0.4.6 never reached the satellite.** It changed no file of the server's update agent, so the agent was rightly not reinstalled and kept the label v0.4.5. The package check compared version labels only, called the agent too old and withheld the package. On 25 Nov the server installed v0.4.6 at 21:54, and the office satellite was still on v0.4.5 the next noon. The check now asks the installer's own question: does this release change the agent's code? If not, the agent is current. If that cannot be proven, the package is still withheld. (#61)
- **A withheld package was silent.** Nothing was logged, no sensor changed and the satellite looked up to date. A refused or unbuildable package is now logged and counted in **Satellites waiting for the server**, with the reason. (#61)
- **A file moved out of a component could go unnoticed.** The server compared two releases with rename detection on, which lists only a moved file's new path. Both paths now count. (#61)
- **TV speech glued to the end of a request.** On the living-room satellite, speech from the TV after you stopped was sent to her with your request (11% of the turns with the TV on, 18–21 Nov; ~2% after v0.4.2). The satellite now ends the turn at your pause, and TV speech never reaches her. (#62)
- **The TV kept the conversation open.** The satellite took continuous TV sound for a voice, so the conversation stayed open (evolution item 25). TV sound no longer holds it, and the caller's own speech is never cut by **Continuous sound cutoff**, so a long dictation survives. (#62)
- **The satellite ended the conversation in the middle of a sentence.** Opening a conversation reset "someone is speaking", and speech that never paused sent no new start, so the silence waits closed over it. Speech begun during her answer was also ignored after the microphone reopened. Both now count. (#59)
- **She ended the conversation as a new sentence began.** Her own close, and the thank-you close, now wait while a sentence begun after her answer is still going. (#59)

## Changes by area
- Satellite program:
  - Ongoing speech holds the conversation open, and her own close waits for a new sentence. (#59)
  - Two beams (caller and TV) for the whole conversation, the caller-or-TV judgement, the satellite ending each turn, and the TV direction calibration. (#62)
  - The journal gets one line per key event (the mode each conversation ran in, re-aims, turn ceilings, possible missed requests, calibration) and one summary per conversation, never per moment of sound. (#62)
- Satellite agent: the two-beam settings, the **Learn TV direction** button and the **TV direction learn** event. (#62)
- Voice gateway (VM): in two-beam conversations the turns come from the satellite; the conversation log records how each turn ended, and a turn with no answer is logged. (#62)
- Server agent:
  - An agent whose code matches a release serves that release's package, even with an older version label. (#61)
  - Withheld packages are logged and listed with the satellites waiting for the server. (#61)
  - Moved files count as changes when deciding what to reinstall. (#61)
  - The voice check, recording only. Its model is downloaded once with `tools/speaker_gate/fetch_model.sh`. (#62)
- Integration:
  - Short help text for **Continuous sound cutoff** (Advanced), with the full behaviour in the repository docs. (#59)
  - The two-beam settings in Advanced, with short help texts; the long version is in `docs/satellite-settings.md`. (#62)
- Repository tooling: a read-only board telemetry logger, a report over it and over the satellite journal, and a guided session script for the living room. (#62)
- **Migrations:** none.

## Known limits
- Until the TV direction is learned, two-beam listening only brings the whole-conversation beam and the satellite ending each turn; it does not yet tell the caller from the TV.
- The voice check records only; nothing is cut by it yet.
- A request that ends before the connection opens (connections measured at 0.2–0.72 s) is not sent.
