# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.6: she lets you finish your sentence

This release stops the satellite from ending a conversation while you are still speaking. It also carries everything in v0.4.5, which is still an alpha.

- A request said in one breath after "Friday" is no longer cut off a few seconds in.
- Speech that began while she was answering counts as you speaking once the microphone reopens.
- When she decides to end the conversation just as you start a new sentence, the satellite keeps listening.
- The **Continuous sound cutoff** setting now explains what it does and what 0 means.

## Fixes
- **The satellite ended the conversation in the middle of a sentence.** On 25 Nov at 18:03 the office satellite heard continuous speech from the word "Friday" until 18:03:17, but closed with the end sound at 18:03:16 as if there were silence. Opening a conversation reset "someone is speaking", and speech that never paused sent no new start, so the 4 s and 5 s waits closed over it. Since 21 Nov, with **Only the wake word interrupts**, speech begun during her answer was also ignored after the microphone reopened. Both now count. (#59)
- **She ended the conversation as a new sentence began.** At 18:12 the same day she closed it 20 ms after you started speaking again. Her own close, and the thank-you close, now wait while a sentence begun after her answer is still going. Sound that was already running when she stopped, such as the TV, does not hold the conversation open. (#59)

## Changes by area
- Satellite program: ongoing speech holds the conversation open, and her own close waits for a new sentence. (#59)
- Integration: help text for **Continuous sound cutoff** (Advanced), including the dictation trade-off of 0. (#59)
- **Migrations:** none.

## Known limits
- With **Continuous sound cutoff** at 0 (no dictation is ever cut), a sound the satellite takes for a voice and that never pauses, such as the TV, can keep a conversation open until she ends it herself. This already happened before this release; it is the next problem to study.
