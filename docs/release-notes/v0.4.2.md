# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.2: only "Friday" interrupts her, and the ring shows when the network is down

This is an **alpha** pre-release. It goes to the alpha satellite, the server and the integration first. Stable devices get it after the 48 h soak and the promotion gate below.

- While she answers, the satellites send no sound to the voice model: only the wake word interrupts her, TV on or off, and the microphone reopens as soon as she finishes.
- On the ReSpeaker XVF3800, the beam lock covers your whole first exchange, on both audio channels.
- The satellite ring turns red after 2 minutes without network, whatever the cause, and flashes red with every error beep.
- Recordings play again on the living room satellite, and Home Assistant follows a satellite that gets a new network address.
- The Server device has a sensor that says when the alpha under test becomes stable.

## New features
- **Only the wake word interrupts her answers.** On a satellite, while she speaks, the microphone is closed to the voice model: TV speech or room talk can no longer cut her answer and end up glued to your request. Saying "Friday" cuts her at once and opens listening, as it already did for reminders and deliveries. The satellite light stays in the "answering" colour while she speaks. The button works as before. This is **on by default**; the switch **Only the wake word interrupts** (general settings, Conversation form of the integration, and `friday.set_setting`) turns it off for every satellite. The web page client is unchanged: there you can still interrupt her by talking (#45).
- **The microphone opens at her stop.** The voice server now tells the satellite when the model marked the answer complete, so the satellite opens the microphone right when she stops talking. When that signal is missing, it waits the same 1.5 s it already waited after a reminder, because answers have pauses in the middle. The sound of that short wait is kept (at most 1.5 s) and sent when the microphone opens, so a "repeat" said right after her does not lose its first syllables. If she goes on talking, the kept sound is thrown away (#45).
- **Beam lock through the first exchange, on both channels.** On the XVF3800, the lock set when you say the wake word now also steers the channel the conversation audio comes from (channel 0), not only channel 1, and it stays until her first answer ends (or the wake word cuts her), instead of releasing at the first pause. If the board does not confirm the change, channel 0 goes back to the factory mode and the lock still holds on channel 1. Closing the conversation, a USB failure, the 90 s ceiling and the program start still release it (#45).
- **Red ring after 2 minutes without network.** Until now the red network light only came on after the satellite had rebooted itself 3 times in 24 h, which never happens when the router or a mesh node is the one down: on 21/11 the living room satellite lost its mesh node and beeped an error at every request with the ring dark. Now the light comes on after 2 minutes without network, whatever the cause, and goes off at most about a minute after the network returns. The network check runs every minute instead of every 5; the recovery steps keep their pace (the first about 5 minutes into the outage, then at most one every 5 minutes), and the 3-reboot cap stays (#46).
- **Red flash with every error beep.** On the XVF3800 ring, every error beep (connection failure, the voice model failing, a conversation that dropped) now also flashes the ring red for 2 s, then it returns to what it showed. The 10 s red of the external error command (updates, setup) is unchanged. No change on the Jabra ring (#46).
- **Alpha promotion sensor.** A new diagnostic sensor on the Server device, **Alpha promotion**. Its state is the time the alpha under test will be promoted to stable, which Home Assistant shows as "in 1 day"; it is "unknown" when no alpha has a running clock (#43).
  - Attributes: `tag`, `status`, `started_at`, `promotes_at`, `soaked_hours` (time since the clock started), `newer_stable` and `alphas` (every alpha still waiting, newest first).
  - Statuses: `waiting_install` (no alpha satellite installed it yet), `soaking` (counting the 48 h), `ready` (promotion in the next cycle), `failed` (never promoted), `promoted` and `superseded` (a newer stable appeared first).
  - Two alphas in a row each keep their own clock; the sensor follows the newest and lists both. With none waiting, it keeps the last one decided, with its result.
- **Conversation log: how your speech arrived.** Each turn line in the conversation log gains `userSpeech`: the number of transcription pieces, the time from the first to the last and the longest pause between them. TV speech glued to the end of a request arrives as pieces after a pause, so this measures the problem from normal use without recording audio (#44).

## Fixes
- **Recordings did not play on satellites adopted with the spoken code.** Every recording of the living room satellite showed "recording not ready" in the activations card. The satellite served recordings only to its local password, while Home Assistant presents the token it issued at adoption; on the office satellite (paired by hand) both are the same, so it worked there. Recordings now accept either. Since 05/11, when adoption with the spoken code arrived (#47).
- **The card said "recording not ready" for everything.** A refused token, an offline satellite and a missing file all looked the same. The card now says "satellite refused", "satellite offline" or "recording not ready" (#47).
- **No-voice rejections of "Sexta-feira" left no recording.** Only Friday's were recorded; on 21/11 the living room satellite rejected "Sexta-feira" without voice 9 times in one afternoon (TV) with no clip. They are now recorded as `sexta_feira_sem_voz_…` clips, like the patience rejections already were, when "Record activations" is on (#47).
- **A satellite that changed address was lost.** Satellites have no reserved address in the router; after a router or mesh reset could give one a new address, the integration kept the old one and the satellite stayed unavailable. When a known satellite (adopted with the spoken code or paired with its id) announces a new address, the integration now updates it and reconnects. The living room satellite will move to its cable address, the one it announces (#47).

## Changes by area
- **Satellite program:**
  - With the switch on, every answer closes the microphone while she speaks, as reminders already did; the wake word still runs during her speech (#45).
  - The pause after her answer is kept and sent when the microphone opens; the beam lock is released when her first answer ends (#45).
  - The network check runs every minute and remembers when the outage began; the ring turns red after 2 minutes, and the recovery steps keep their 5-minute pace (#46).
  - Every error beep flashes the ring red for 2 s (#46).
  - No-voice rejections of the extra words are recorded (#47).
- **Satellite agent:**
  - The **Only the wake word interrupts** setting reaches the program (#45).
  - Recordings accept the token Home Assistant uses (#47).
- **Voice gateway (VM):** tells the satellite when her answer is complete, and the conversation log records how your speech arrived (#44, #45).
- **Server agent:** publishes the Alpha promotion sensor on every update cycle; promotion itself is unchanged (#43).
- **Integration:**
  - **Only the wake word interrupts** joins the general settings, in English and pt-BR (#45).
  - The activations card says whether a recording is missing, refused or the satellite is offline (#47).
  - A known satellite that announces a new address is updated and reconnected (#47).
- **Packaging:** the network check timer runs every minute; a rollback restores the 5-minute one (#46).
- **Wake word training:** the extra words' no-voice clips enter the harvest, behind the same word guards as the near misses (#47).
- **Migrations:** none.

## Known limits
- Interrupting her without saying "Friday" no longer works on satellites while the switch is on (decision of the owner, 21/11).
- TV speech that enters the follow-up window after the microphone reopens is not handled here (about 6 turns in the measured data); a speaker gate is the next step.
