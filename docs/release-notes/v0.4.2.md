# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.2: only "Friday" interrupts her answers, and Home Assistant shows when the alpha will be promoted

This is an **alpha** pre-release. It goes to the alpha satellite, the server and the integration first. Stable devices get it after the 48 h soak and the promotion gate below.

- While she answers, the satellites no longer send any sound to the voice model. Only the wake word interrupts her, with or without the TV on.
- The microphone opens again as soon as her answer is complete, and the first words of a "repeat" said right after her are kept.
- On the ReSpeaker XVF3800 satellites, the beam lock now covers the whole first exchange (your request and her answer), on both audio channels.
- The Server device has a new sensor that says when the alpha being tested will become stable, and why it has not yet if something is in the way.

## New features
- **Only the wake word interrupts her answers.** On a satellite, while she speaks, the microphone is closed to the voice model: TV speech or room talk can no longer cut her answer and end up glued to your request. Saying "Friday" cuts her at once and opens listening, as it already did for reminders and deliveries. The satellite light stays in the "answering" colour while she speaks. The button works as before. This is **on by default**; the switch **Only the wake word interrupts** (general settings, Conversation form of the integration, and `friday.set_setting`) turns it off for every satellite. The web page client is unchanged: there you can still interrupt her by talking.
- **The microphone opens at her stop.** The voice server now tells the satellite when the model marked the answer complete, so the satellite opens the microphone right when she stops talking. When that signal is missing, it waits the same 1.5 s it already waited after a reminder, because answers have pauses in the middle. The sound of that short wait is kept (at most 1.5 s) and sent when the microphone opens, so a "repeat" said right after her does not lose its first syllables. If she goes on talking, the kept sound is thrown away.
- **Beam lock through the first exchange, on both channels.** On the XVF3800, the lock set when you say the wake word now also steers the channel the conversation audio comes from (channel 0), not only channel 1, and it stays until her first answer ends (or the wake word cuts her), instead of releasing at the first pause. If the board does not confirm the change, channel 0 goes back to the factory mode and the lock still holds on channel 1. Closing the conversation, a USB failure, the 90 s ceiling and the program start still release it.
- **Alpha promotion sensor.** A new diagnostic sensor on the Server device, **Alpha promotion**. Its state is the time the alpha under test will be promoted to stable, which Home Assistant shows as "in 1 day". It is "unknown" when the alpha has no running clock (not installed yet, or failed) or when there is no alpha to track.
- **Attributes.** `tag` (the release tracked), `status`, `started_at`, `promotes_at`, `soaked_hours` (hours since the clock started, or until the promotion once it happened), `newer_stable` (a stable release newer than the tag already exists) and `alphas` (every alpha still waiting, newest first, each with its own status and promotion time).
- **Statuses.** `waiting_install` (no alpha satellite has installed it yet, so there is no clock), `soaking` (counting the 48 h), `ready` (48 h passed, the promotion happens in the next 10-minute cycle or on the next GitHub retry), `failed` (an alpha device reported a failure; it is never promoted), `promoted` and `superseded` (a stable release newer than it appeared first).
- **Two alphas in a row each keep their own clock.** The sensor follows the newest one and lists both in `alphas`. An alpha older than the newest stable release drops out of the list, even one that no satellite ever installed: it can only end `superseded`. When no alpha is waiting, the sensor keeps showing the last one decided, with its result.
- Instead of "hours remaining", the state is the promotion time itself (Home Assistant renders the remaining time) and `soaked_hours` is the elapsed time.
- **Conversation log: how your speech arrived.** Each turn line in the conversation log gains `userSpeech`: the number of transcription pieces, the time from the first to the last and the longest pause between them. TV speech glued to the end of a request arrives as pieces after a pause, so this measures the problem from normal use without recording audio (#44).

## Changes by area
- **Satellite program:** with the switch on, answers are now "recados" (the microphone closes while she speaks; only the wake word cuts). New maestro actions `guardar_pausa` (hold the sound of the pause in the connection, at most 1.5 s, sent when the microphone opens) and `fim_da_resposta` (the first answer ended: release the beam lock); new event `resposta_completa`; config key `only_wake_word_interrupts` (default on). The echo meter keeps measuring while she speaks, with the microphone closed. The wake word inference runs while she speaks, as it already did for recados.
- **Satellite agent:** hidden switch descriptor **Only the wake word interrupts** (`only_wake_word_interrupts`), mapped to the program config.
- **Voice gateway (VM):** forwards the model's `turn_complete` to the satellite as the RTVI server message `resposta_completa`; turn lines carry `userSpeech`. The web page client is unchanged.
- **Integration:** `only_wake_word_interrupts` joins the general settings (Conversation form, English and pt-BR labels, `friday.set_setting`); sent to every satellite like the other general settings. The Alpha promotion sensor is built from the server's descriptor, with no code change.
- **Server agent (VM host):** new `Orchestrator.alpha_promotion()` and the descriptor `ota_alpha_promotion`, pushed with the other OTA entities on every OTA cycle (no timer of its own, so `soaked_hours` moves about every 6 minutes at most). The newest-stable computation of the promotion step moved into one helper shared with the sensor; promotion behavior is unchanged.
- No change to the store format and no migration.

## Known limits
- Interrupting her without saying "Friday" no longer works on satellites while the switch is on (decision of the owner, 21/11).
- TV speech that enters the follow-up window after the microphone reopens is not handled here (about 6 turns in the measured data); a speaker gate is the next step.

## Promotion gate (stable)
- [ ] MK1 (alpha) installs v0.4.2 and **Alpha promotion** shows `soaking` for v0.4.2, with a promotion time 48 h after the install. Before the install, the status is `waiting_install` and the state is "unknown".
- [ ] While she answers there is no audio underrun and her voice is not robotic (the wake word runs during her speech, as it already did for reminders; the CPU skip of 06/10 existed for this).
- [ ] "Friday" said while she speaks cuts her at once and opens listening; a "repeat" said right after her answer keeps its start.
- [ ] "Friday, <request>" said in one breath while she speaks: she stops and her answer to the new request is heard.
- [ ] Her own voice saying her name in an answer does not cut her.
- [ ] With the switch off, everything behaves as in v0.4.1 (you can interrupt her by talking; reminders and deliveries as before).
- [ ] The satellite journal shows `AUDIO_MGR_OP_L 6 0` accepted and read back after the wake word (no `canal 0 ficou no 8 0` line); the level of channel 0 while locked is close to the factory level (if AGC does not apply there, adjust before promotion).
- [ ] After 48 h, the sensor shows `promoted` for v0.4.2 and the release is stable on GitHub.
