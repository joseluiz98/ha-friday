# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.8: a volume slider with no dead spots, and "Friday" heard over music

This release gives every point of the satellite volume its own loudness and makes the office satellite hear the wake word while music plays.

- Every percent of the volume changes the sound, for voice and music, and the slider stays where you leave it.
- The XVF3800 satellite uses its own 1 dB steps.
- With music playing, a Jabra satellite no longer rejects most real "Friday" calls.

## New features
- **Fine volume.** The satellite volume was a hardware step, 12 positions about 9 points apart: moving the slider inside a step changed neither the sound nor the ring, and the slider then jumped to the step's own percent. The board now sits on its own level at or above the asked loudness and a new software control attenuates the rest. (#PR)
  - The percents the old steps used (9, 18, 27 … 91, 100%) sound as before. Below 9% the volume now keeps going down, 4 dB under the old minimum; it never mutes.
  - The XVF3800 uses its 61 hardware levels of 1 dB instead of 12.
  - Voice ("volume 7", "louder") and the Jabra buttons work as before. The Jabra ring still shows steps.
- **Wake word tolerant to music.** While the satellite's music player is playing, and for 5 s after, the personal voice check is set aside and the speech gate is relaxed. On by default on Jabra satellites, off on XVF3800; an Advanced switch overrides it. (#63)

## Fixes
- **The same percent could jump back.** The satellite reported the step's percent, not the one asked. It now keeps and reports the exact percent, and a slow status read can no longer publish an older value over a newer one. (#PR)

## Changes by area
- Satellite program:
  - Exact volume percent, with the board level and the new fine control written in an order that never overshoots. (#PR)
  - The old volume webhook to Home Assistant is gone; a leftover setting is ignored. (#PR)
  - Relaxed wake word checks while music plays. (#63)
- Satellite agent:
  - The volume number reports and sets the exact percent. (#PR)
  - The **Wake word tolerant to music** setting. (#63)
- Integration: the **Wake word tolerant to music** switch in Advanced. (#63)
- Image: new satellites carry the fine volume audio files, and the image check requires them. (#PR)
- **Migrations:** `0009_fine_volume` installs the two audio files and restarts the music player.

## Known limits
- After a rollback, voice and music can differ by up to 4 dB until the music player restarts.
- The XVF3800 steps were checked against the levels the board declares, not yet with a microphone.
