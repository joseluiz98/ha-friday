# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.5: timers that only ring, and the right satellite answers when two hear you

This release makes timers ring Echo style, without opening a conversation, and gives the choice between two satellites a little more time to hear the second one. It also carries everything in v0.4.4, which had not been promoted yet.

- A timer only rings. No conversation opens at expiry, and after three beep loops she says the timer's name once.
- "Friday, parar" stops every ringing timer on every satellite, also by room or by name.
- When two satellites hear you, the one that hears you best answers more often: the choice waits 50 ms longer, and every claim that still arrives late is now recorded.
- Transcribing wake-word clips for analysis and retraining now uses the larger speech model, which no longer mistakes "Friday" for TV dialogue.

## New features
- **Timers ring Echo style.** (#57)
  - The ring uses the existing ramp, then beep loops, and after three loops one spoken notice with the timer's name. The cap stays 300 s.
  - The ring is purple at 40 %, pulsing fast. Do-not-disturb is unchanged.
  - When a named timer is created, the assistant prepares "O timer <nome> terminou." and sends it to the satellite. A generic clip that ships with the satellite covers unnamed timers and any failure.
  - After the wake word, "parar" stops every ringing timer on every satellite, also by room or by name, and she says which one ended. The button also stops a paused timer.
- **A problem sensor for failing speech.** Three text-to-speech failures in a row, announcements included, raise `tts_failing`; it clears at the next good one and expires after 7 days. (#57)

## Fixes
- **The satellite that hears you best could lose the choice to a slower one.** On 24 Nov at 21:51 both satellites heard "Friday", but the office satellite's claim reached the server first, the round closed with one candidate and the living room satellite, which heard the voice louder, was told it was late. In the eight days measured, the 24 rounds with two satellites closed in 27 to 194 ms, right against the old 200 ms limit. The window is now 250 ms (satellites up to v0.4.4 give up at 300 ms, so it cannot go much higher until they all update), and each late claim is written to the arbitration log with its delay, score and voice level, so the next window is chosen from data. (#58)
- **Analysis and retraining labelled TV dialogue wrongly.** The small speech model heard "O que é isso?" where the larger one heard "Friday." The analysis and the training harvest now use the larger model. (#58)

## Changes by area
- Satellite program: ring, spoken notice and stop-by-voice for timers; purple pulsing ring. (#57)
- Voice gateway (VM):
  - Spoken timer notice prepared at creation and sent to the satellite; timers stop on every satellite. (#57)
  - Text-to-speech failure streak and its problem sensor. (#57)
  - 250 ms choice window between satellites and a record of late claims. (#58)
- Image: generic timer clip ships in the image and the update package. (#57)
- Wake word training: analysis and harvest transcribe with the larger model. (#58)
- **Migrations:** none.
