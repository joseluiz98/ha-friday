# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.5: voice learning works on every satellite from day one

This release makes the voice-learning program train on any satellite, including a new one, and stops the assistant from describing how the satellite works inside. It also carries everything in v0.4.4, which had not been promoted yet.

- The voice-learning program no longer needs the October archive that only the office satellite has.
- A satellite without enough ambient recording of its own borrows negatives from the others the house already knows.
- The assistant no longer says things like "the satellite will conduct the process".

## Fixes
- **Voice learning on the living room satellite recorded everything and then failed.** On 23/11 the training stopped with `FileNotFoundError` on `rotulos.json`, a file from the 15/10 archive that exists only on the office satellite, and the voice was not learned. The training now reads it only when it exists, and takes its negatives from the satellite's own ambient recording. (#55)
- **The assistant explained the machinery.** The wording of the voice-learning tools made it say "o satélite conduzirá o processo". The tool texts now describe what the person lives (beeps, moving, free speech) and the system instruction says the person perceives one assistant. (#55)

## New features
- **Baseline of negatives for new satellites.** With less than 30 minutes of its own ambient audio, a satellite asks the server for negatives from the other satellites (their ambient recordings and the false activations they were taught) when the training starts. The pack never includes anyone's recorded voice. With neither its own audio nor a baseline, the training is refused with a message that says what is missing. (#55)

## Changes by area
- Satellite program: negatives from own ambient or baseline, minimum of negatives before approval, missing archive no longer an error. (#55)
- Satellite agent: fetches and unpacks the baseline for the program. (#55)
- Server agent: `GET /v1/training/negatives` on the satellites' TLS port. (#55)
- Voice gateway (VM): voice-learning, end-conversation and answer-by-Telegram texts without the machinery; one-assistant fact in the system instruction. (#55)
- **Migrations:** none.
