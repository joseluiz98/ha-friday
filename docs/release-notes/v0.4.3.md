# F.R.I.D.A.Y. 0.4.3: satellites stay connected on the cable, and you can hold an alpha back

This release fixes the v0.4.2 regression that left satellites unavailable and lets you stop an alpha from becoming stable.

- A satellite with both a cable and Wi-Fi no longer makes Home Assistant reload the integration, which left the other satellites unavailable.
- Satellites talk to Home Assistant over the cable when it is plugged in, switch to the Wi-Fi at once when it is not, and go back to the cable by themselves.
- A new switch on the Server device stops the alpha under test from being promoted to stable.

## New features
- **Hold an alpha back from stable.** The Server device has a new switch, **Alpha promotion**. (#49)
  - It follows the newest alpha that can still become stable. Turn it off and that alpha is not promoted when its 48 h end; turn it back on and it is promoted at the next update check.
  - The `friday.set_alpha_promotion` action does the same for any alpha by its version (`tag`), so intermediate alphas can be held one by one.
  - An alpha that failed its soak on its own (rollback) stays failed; allowing it again is refused.
  - The **Alpha promotion** sensor shows `blocked`, and every block or allow goes to the logbook.

## Fixes
- **Satellites went unavailable after a satellite announced its other address.** Since v0.4.2, Home Assistant followed a satellite that announced a new address by reloading the whole integration. The living room satellite answers on its cable and on its Wi-Fi, so on 21/11 it "moved" twice in 10 s; the two reloads ran together and left the office satellite's entities duplicated and unavailable until Home Assistant restarted. Now the satellite declares both addresses, the cable is preferred, and an address change only reconnects that satellite. (#48)
- **The activations card listed the same activation many times.** Each of those reloads, and every Home Assistant restart, made the activation entities pass through unavailable and come back with their last event, and the card showed each return as a new row: on 22/11 one office activation appeared 17 times. The card now shows each event once. (#48)

## Changes by area
- Satellite agent: announces its cable and Wi-Fi addresses. (#48)
- Server agent: Alpha promotion switch and hand block of a promotion, checked again right before promoting. (#49)
- Integration:
  - Satellite connection on the cable first, Wi-Fi fallback, back to the cable after 60 s of answers; no reload on address changes. (#48)
  - Activations card shows each event once. (#48)
  - `friday.set_alpha_promotion` action. (#49)
- Migrations: none.

## Promotion gate (stable)
- [ ] Office satellite (Wi-Fi only): no "Satellite ... addresses" lines in the Home Assistant log over 24 h, and all its entities stay available across a Home Assistant restart.
- [ ] System Health activations card: over 24 h, each activation appears once, also after a Home Assistant restart.
- [ ] Server device: **Alpha promotion** is on for this alpha; turning it off makes the sensor show `blocked`; turning it back on clears it.

After promotion (stable satellites only):
- [ ] Living room satellite connects on the cable; unplugging the cable moves it to the Wi-Fi within seconds with no reload, and plugging it back returns to the cable after about a minute.
